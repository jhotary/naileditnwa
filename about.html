Why Nailed It Roofing?

Locally owned, based in Bentonville, and built on the principle that honesty matters more than the next sale.

🤝

Integrity First. Always.

We built this company on one rule: tell the truth, even when it costs us a job. We won't recommend a full replacement when a repair will do. We won't encourage you to file an insurance claim if your roof doesn't qualify.

  • Honest assessments — we'll tell you if you don't have damage
  • No high-pressure sales tactics. Ever.
  • Transparent pricing with written estimates before any work starts
  • State certified, bonded, and insured
  • We treat your home like it's our own
🧠

Deep Industry Knowledge

Roofing is more than hammers and shingles. Understanding building codes, manufacturer specifications, insurance policy language, and adjuster processes is what separates a good contractor from a great one.

  • Xactimate-certified estimating — carrier-accepted documentation
  • EagleView aerial measurements for accuracy on every job
  • In-depth knowledge of insurance claim process & supplements
  • GAF & Owens Corning certified installation standards
  • Up-to-date on NWA building codes & permit requirements
⚡

Speed That Protects Your Home

When your roof is compromised, every day it stays that way is a day your home is at risk. Water damage doesn't wait. We move fast — from initial assessment to job completion — without cutting corners on quality.

  • Same-day or next-day assessment scheduling
  • Emergency tarping available within hours of a storm
  • Most residential replacements completed in 1–2 days
  • Full job photo documentation so you see every step

"We started Nailed It Roofing because we saw too many homeowners get taken advantage of — by out-of-state storm chasers, by contractors who encouraged unnecessary claims, and by companies that disappeared after taking a deposit. We do things differently. Our name is on every job, and we live in the same neighborhoods we work in. That accountability matters to us."
